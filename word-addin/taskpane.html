ScholarKit

Click a phrase to insert it at your cursor. Replace the [bracketed] parts with your own content.

Add the PDFs, Word docs, or text files you are citing. ScholarKit checks your draft against them for overlapping passages. Everything stays in your browser.

    To clear highlights afterward: select all text in Word, then Home > Text Highlight Color > No Color.

    Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and is sent only to Google's Gemini API. The Phrases and Similarity tabs work without a key.
